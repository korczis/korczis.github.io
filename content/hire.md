A coding agent can write a feature in minutes; what it cannot do on its own is show that the feature is finished. That gap is the work I am hired for. [Majordomus](@/glossary/majordomus.md) is my answer to it in the open: one policy for every [AI agent](@/glossary/ai-agent.md), tasks scoped like contracts, and work that lands only when its checks ran. The same discipline goes into agent tooling over [MCP](@/glossary/mcp.md), into production backends in [Elixir](@/glossary/elixir.md) and [Rust](@/glossary/rust.md) on [PostgreSQL](@/glossary/postgresql.md), and into the data behind decisions.

That last part is where engineering meets money. In [due diligence](@/glossary/due-diligence.md) for [mergers and acquisitions](@/glossary/mergers-and-acquisitions.md) and in [OSINT](@/glossary/osint.md) investigations, a finding without its source is worthless, so the systems I build keep the source next to every answer.

Everything stated on this page is either a fact the owner wrote or a measurement with its date, and most of the code behind it is [open source](@/glossary/open-source.md): read the repositories before the interview, not after it.
